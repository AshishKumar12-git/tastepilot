# ✦ TastePilot

### Discover experiences that match your taste.

TastePilot is an AI-powered discovery application that helps people find places, restaurants, entertainment, and experiences based on their interests, preferences, location, and social context.

### The Problem

Finding something to do is easy. Finding something that genuinely matches your taste is not.

Traditional discovery platforms often rely on popularity, ratings, or generic recommendations. But what people enjoy is influenced by their interests, cultural preferences, companions, and the experiences they are looking for.

TastePilot aims to make discovery more personal by understanding what users are looking for and helping them explore experiences that better match their preferences.

### Our Approach

Users can describe what they want in natural language, just as they would ask a friend for recommendations.

TastePilot interprets the request, identifies relevant preferences, and uses Qloo's cultural intelligence to discover and rank relevant experiences. An evaluation workflow helps assess recommendation quality, while conversational responses make the results easier to understand.

### What Makes TastePilot Different?

TastePilot combines natural-language understanding with Qloo's cultural taste graph to move beyond generic recommendations.

Rather than focusing only on what's popular, it aims to help users discover experiences that align with their individual tastes and the context of their plans.

### Built With

Python · Streamlit · NVIDIA Nemotron · Qloo Insights API
